ML Group Project — Team of 4

Human Activity Recognition

Developed an end-to-end machine learning pipeline to identify six human activities from multivariate time-series data collected through a wearable sensor system.

Extracted over 1,470 features per sample including statistical, frequency-domain FFT, autocorrelation, and cross-sensor correlation features. Achieved a Macro-F1 of 0.797 ± 0.021 using a tuned LightGBM model with stratified 5-fold cross-validation.

PythonNumPyPandasScikit-learnLightGBMKaggle
